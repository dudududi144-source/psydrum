What it is

Pure HOW layer

A PsyDevice that only realizes sound. It never composes, never schedules, never owns transport — content arrives as events from the host.

Hybrid ACB bank

Pre-rendered analog-circuit-behavior buffers (velocity layers × round-robin variants) blended over real-time synthesis.

Drum-native behavior

Choke groups, per-drum tone envelopes, velocity-to-timbre, and anti-machine-gun variance — the things a sampler voice can't express.

Deterministic by construction

Seeded variance everywhere it's allowed; pitch, choke, routing and drop policy never vary. Same seed → same render.
